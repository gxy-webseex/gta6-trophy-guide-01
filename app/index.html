⚠ Watch these 4 — they can be missed

There is no mission replay in GTA VI, and the published guides flag only one trophy as potentially missable. This guide also flags the two partner trophies and the choice trophy, because they need your partner present or a story choice. Do them when the moment appears; everything else can be finished in free roam after the story.
